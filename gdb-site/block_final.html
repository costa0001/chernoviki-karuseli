FAQ

Questions clients ask first

How much does it cost?

Each plan has a fixed monthly price that depends on how much content you need. You get an exact quote after the free audit, before you commit to anything.

When will I see results?

The profile makeover changes the first impression immediately. Reach and engagement usually grow within the first 4–6 weeks of regular posting. A steady flow of enquiries builds over 2–3 months.

Do I have to film myself or be on camera?

No. We can build content around your products, your place and your team. If you are happy to appear, short videos with you usually perform even better, and we guide you through it.

How much of my time does it take?

About one hour a month: a short call to plan, a shoot at your place, and approving content in a shared calendar.

Which businesses do you work with?

Restaurants and cafés, beauty and hair salons, clinics, fitness studios, shops, consultants and other service businesses that want more clients from social media.

In which languages do you post?

English, French and Russian. We match the language of your clients.

Who owns the account?

You do, always. We work as collaborators on your account and never change ownership.

Is there a long-term contract?

We work month to month. We would rather keep you with results than with a contract.
